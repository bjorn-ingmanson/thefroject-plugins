# Employee Data Protection Rules

- Never include employee names, salaries, performance ratings, or health information in documents beyond their intended audience
- Treat personnel cases (disciplinary, grievance, medical leave) as restricted — named individuals only
- Use role or team labels instead of names when analyzing people data ("a senior engineer" not the person's name)
- Check the lawful basis before processing personal data for a new purpose — consent given for one use does not cover another
- Respect data-subject rights: employees can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data
- Apply retention limits — candidate data, exit records, and old reviews should not be kept indefinitely
- Never move employee data into tools or documents outside the approved HR systems without checking first
- Anonymize or aggregate data in reports, dashboards, and examples — a "team of 3" breakdown can identify individuals

**Examples of violations:**
- Pasting a performance review excerpt into a shared planning doc → summarize the theme without the individual
- Keeping rejected candidate CVs "just in case" past the retention period → delete on schedule
- A salary benchmarking sheet with names shared to a team channel → aggregate by level and role
